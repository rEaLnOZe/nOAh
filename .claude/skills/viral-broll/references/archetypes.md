# The three archetypes

Each is the same clean style (see `style-dna.md`) with a different **hero**. Open
the matching frame in `assets/reference-frames/` while authoring — it's the target.

---

## 1. Object-centered — `01-object-centered_save-time.png`

**Hero:** one object/prop/metaphor, dead center, large.
**Use when:** the speaker names a concrete thing you can picture (a tool, product,
metaphor prop — "hourglass", "a lock", "the funnel", "a trophy").

Reference frame: a golden **hourglass** centered, the words **"SAVE TIME"** as a
huge light-gray watermark behind it, two **alarm clocks** tucked into the top-left
and bottom-right corners (partly cropped), a thin curved pen-line across the frame,
a blue accent (card border glow + a scatter of blue). White shaded background.

Layout recipe:
- Hero object centered, ~45–60% of frame height, with the black-blur grounding
  shadow beneath it.
- Watermark: the beat's key word(s) in huge heavy type, ~15% opacity, behind the
  hero, bleeding off the edges.
- 2 supporting props in opposite corners, related to the object, partly off-frame.
- 1 pen-curve line for texture; optional dash row.
- Accent: topic color if any, else brand orange — on the card glow + maybe one prop.

Motion: object zooms in (`60→100`, cubic ease) with the grounding shadow tracking
it; corner props slide in from their edges, staggered; pen-line wipes on; final
compound zoom `100→120` + ~8° rotation.

## 2. Character-based — `02-character-based_whatsapp.png`

**Hero:** a person or brand figure, centered on a magazine-style card.
**Use when:** the speaker names a person or a company that has a face/logo
("Zuckerberg", "the CEO", "WhatsApp", "OpenAI", "our founder").

Reference frame: **Mark Zuckerberg** cut out and placed on a green-tinted panel
inside a rounded card, **"WHATSAPP"** as the brand headline + a small magazine
"owned by / Mark Zuckerberg" credit and micro body-copy, the **WhatsApp logo**
scattered top-left and bottom-right, a green ✦ badge, halftone-dot dark background.
Green accent throughout (it's WhatsApp).

Layout recipe:
- Character cut out (bg removed), placed on a colored rounded panel — **let part of
  the subject break out of the box** (head/shoulders overflow the top edge); don't
  fully contain them. This overlap is what makes it pop.
- Magazine scaffolding: a bold brand/name **headline**, a one-line role/credit
  ("owned by …", "CEO of …"), a couple of lines of light micro body-copy.
- Brand watermark word repeated faintly behind; brand logo scattered in 2 corners.
- Accent = the brand's color (this archetype almost always has one).

Motion: character zooms/settles into the box (cubic-out) with the grounding shadow;
a rectangle mask reveals them "popping out"; headline + credit type/slide in;
logos fade in; final compound zoom `100→125`.

Sourcing note: the cutout person/logo is a real asset. If asset sourcing is blocked
(no web/stock), fall back to text-centered for that beat rather than shipping an
empty character card — an honest text hit beats a broken portrait.

---

## 3. Text-centered — `03-text-centered_hesitation.png`

**Hero:** a punchy phrase — the words *are* the subject.
**Use when:** the speaker lands a claim, hook, thesis, or one-liner with no single
object/person to show ("Hesitation steals your biggest opportunities", "Most people
quit here", a bold stat phrased as a sentence).

Reference frame: **"Hesitation STEALS your BIGGEST opportunities"** stacked in the
middle in mixed weights/sizes, **"opportunities"** in *italic red* (the one accent
word), an alarm clock top-left and a gold **trophy** bottom, diagonal repeated
"KEEP OUT / DON'T TOUCH" caution ribbon for urgency, white shaded background, red
accent.

Layout recipe:
- The phrase is the hero, center, stacked across 2–4 lines with **deliberate size /
  weight contrast** — small connective words, huge key words. Exactly one word in
  *italic accent* (the emotional payload).
- Supporting: 1–2 props that reinforce the emotion (clock = time/urgency, trophy =
  win), a texture ribbon or pen-curve, faint watermark of the key word behind.
- Accent: the emotional color (red urgency, gold win, orange brand default).

Motion: words reveal in reading order with a mask-rise or type-on, the accent word
punches (scale overshoot to ~1.06 + color), props slide in, final compound zoom
`100→120`. This is also the default for **hooks and CTAs** (see detection.md).

---

## Choosing between them (quick tie-breakers)

- Speaker names a **thing** you can show → object. A **who** → character. Neither,
  just a strong **sentence** → text.
- A **hook** (first strong claim, first ~3s) or **CTA/closer** → text-centered.
- A beat has both an object and a claim → prefer the one the sentence is *about*.
  ("Hesitation steals opportunities" is about the claim → text, even though a clock
  appears.)
- Asset can't be sourced for object/character → downgrade to text-centered.
